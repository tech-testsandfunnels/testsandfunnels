From brief to finished media

  1. 1

    Brief

    We start from the goal, the audience and the claim you want to make, and list where each piece will run.

  2. 2

    Produce

    We shoot, brief creators or generate visuals to the plan, producing in bulk so one session covers the list.

  3. 3

    Review

    Every piece is checked against your brand, the advertising rules and its usage rights before it goes live.

  4. 4

    Cut and deliver

    We edit into the cut-downs and crops each placement needs and deliver files ready to upload.

Rights, disclosure and review

  • Usage rights are agreed in the brief, including where and for how long content can run as an ad.
  • Paid collaborations are disclosed under ASCI's influencer advertising guidelines.
  • AI-generated visuals and video are labelled as AI-generated and reviewed by a person before they run.
  • Client work is shown only with the client's approval. Patient and customer details are blurred or left out.
  • For hospitals and clinics: no patient footage and no treatment-outcome claims.

Questions about our media work

What is in the t&f media portfolio?

It covers the media our production house makes: product and lifestyle photography, brand films and ad films, UGC, influencer content, AI visuals and video, and personal-brand reels.

Can I see work from my industry?

Mention your industry when you book your Free Business Audit and we will share relevant samples that clients have approved for sharing.

Can this media run as paid ads?

Yes. Usage rights, including where and for how long content can run as an ad, are agreed in the brief. Creator content runs as an ad with the creator's permission, and paid collaborations are disclosed.

Do you label AI-generated media?

Yes. AI-generated visuals and video are labelled as AI-generated and reviewed by a person before they run.
