Outreach agent: cold outreach that starts real conversations

Our AI outreach agent builds prospect lists around your ideal customer, writes personalised first messages and follow-ups on email, WhatsApp and social DMs, and logs every reply, so your team spends its time on prospects who answered.

Our outreach agent runs B2B cold outreach: it sources prospects that match your ideal customer, sends personalised messages and follow-ups on email, WhatsApp and social DMs within each channel's rules, and logs replies in your CRM, aiming for booked demos. Outreach that stays within the rules

  • Each channel's rules are followed, including WhatsApp's business messaging rules.
  • Every message gives a way to opt out, and opt-outs are honoured straight away.
  • Where each contact came from is recorded. Check your consent notice against India's DPDP Act with your own adviser.
  • You approve the segments, the message templates and a sample of messages before launch.

Questions about the outreach agent

How personal are the messages an outreach agent sends?

Each first message is written for the segment and refers to something specific about the prospect's business. You approve the templates and a sample before anything is sent.

What happens when a prospect replies?

The reply is logged in your CRM and the sequence stops for that contact. Interested prospects move to follow-up, and conversations that need a person are passed to one.
