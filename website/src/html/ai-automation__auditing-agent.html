Questions about the auditing agent

Is the Free Business Audit done by an AI agent?

The agent runs the checks and drafts the findings. A person at t&f collects your brief first, reviews the findings, adds what only a conversation can uncover and presents the strategy.

What access does the auditing agent need?

Public checks, such as search, AI answers, social profiles and your website, need none. For ad accounts, analytics, Tag Manager or a CRM we ask for read access, and only where the audit needs it.
