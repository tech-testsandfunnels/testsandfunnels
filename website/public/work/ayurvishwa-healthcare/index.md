# Ayurvishwa Healthcare: 815 enquiries and 77 patient visits in April 2025

A healthcare marketing case study: Meta lead campaigns for the clinics and visiting-doctor days of an Ayurveda provider in Maharashtra, reported by clinic from the first enquiry to the patient who walked in.

t&f ran Meta lead campaigns for the clinics and visiting-doctor days of Ayurvishwa Healthcare, an Ayurveda provider in Maharashtra. The campaigns logged 815 enquiries and 77 patient visits in April 2025, per t&f's own campaign reports. No patient details are used or shown.

## Results

**815** Enquiries logged in April 2025 across visiting-doctor and standalone clinic campaigns

**221** Enquiries marked qualified, with 69 more marked potential

**77** Patients visited, 14 of them for a consultation only

**₹8.10** Lowest cost per lead on a single ad, a Mumbai clinic Reel in May

Source: tests&funnels' own campaign reports for Ayurvishwa Healthcare. The enquiry, qualification and patient-visit figures are the final summary of the April 2025 report for visiting-doctor and standalone clinic campaigns. Cost per lead is as Meta reported it for each ad in the monthly ad reports for May to July. These are totals only. No patient details are used or shown.

## How the 815 enquiries were sorted

A lead total alone says little about how many people can be booked, so the report shows the status of every enquiry. A little over one in four was marked qualified, and more than four in ten had no status recorded.

| Status in the April report | Enquiries |
|----------------------------|-----------|
| Qualified (yes)            | 221       |
| Qualified (potential)      | 69        |
| Not specific               | 168       |
| Blank, no status recorded  | 357       |
| **Total**                  | **815**   |

## Cost per lead, ad by ad

The May to July ad reports list click-through rate and cost per lead for every ad. The spread between ads is wide.

| Campaign group                                                                              | Cost per lead, lowest to highest ad |
|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Mumbai standalone clinic ads                                                                | ₹8.10 to ₹16.22                     |
| Pune standalone clinic ads                                                                  | ₹8.74 to ₹19.10                     |
| Visiting-doctor town ads (Solapur, Satara, Sangli, Nashik, Sambhajinagar, Amravati, Nanded) | ₹8.29 to ₹67.88                     |

Cost per lead is the cost to get an enquiry, not the cost to get a patient. The highest ad cost more than eight times the lowest, which is why the reports break cost down by ad and by clinic.

## Questions about this case study

### What did tests&funnels do for Ayurvishwa Healthcare?

We ran Meta lead campaigns for its clinics and visiting-doctor days, tracked enquiries through to patient visits, planned Google Ads keywords and ad copy, audited its website for SEO and made ad and social creatives.

### What is a visiting OPD campaign?

In the campaign reports, a visiting OPD is an outpatient day a doctor holds at a branch on a set date, and a standalone OPD is a fixed clinic. A visiting-OPD campaign advertises the day in that town so people can enquire before it.

### Does this case study claim any treatment results?

No. It reports enquiries, qualified enquiries, patient visits and cost per lead. It makes no claim about health outcomes, and it shows no patient data.

### Why were only 221 of 815 enquiries marked qualified?

357 enquiries had no status recorded and 168 were marked not specific. We show the full split because a lead total on its own overstates how many people can be booked.
