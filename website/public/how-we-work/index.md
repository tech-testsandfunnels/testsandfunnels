# How we work: four steps from brief to roadmap

You always know what happens next. Our marketing agency process starts from your brief, not a pitch.

Every engagement follows four steps: we collect your brief, audit your industry and full funnel, pitch a strategy and action plan, and share the prerequisites and the roadmap for onboarding and execution.

## The four steps

| Step                          | What we do                                                                                                              | What you get                                     |
|-------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| 1\. Brief                     | We collect a brief from you, in your words.                                                                             | A completed client brief                         |
| 2\. Industry and client audit | We audit your industry and your full funnel, from visibility through lead capture, nurturing, management and retention. | An audit report showing the gaps                 |
| 3\. Strategy and action plan  | We pitch the strategy and action plan that fixes the gaps, including which manual steps our agents take over first.     | A strategy presentation and action plan          |
| 4\. Prerequisites and roadmap | We share what we need from you, and the roadmap for onboarding and executing the strategy.                              | A prerequisites checklist and onboarding roadmap |

## The full-funnel audit

| Audit part   | What it checks                                                        |
|--------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Industry     | Market, competitors, customer segments and benchmark metrics.         |
| Visibility   | Search (SEO, AEO, GEO), paid ads, social presence and local listings. |
| Lead capture | Landing pages, forms, offers, tracking and conversion events.         |
| Nurturing    | Email and WhatsApp follow-up, automations and response time.          |
| Management   | Lead handling, CRM, follow-up process and reporting.                  |
| Retention    | Repeat purchase or re-engagement, reviews and loyalty.                |

For each stage, the audit also marks which steps are still done by hand and could move to an agent.

## What we typically need from you

- Access to your ad accounts, analytics and Tag Manager.
- Access to your store, website or CRM.
- Your brand assets and guidelines.
- Past campaign and sales data, if you have it.
- One person on your side who can approve work.

The exact list for your business comes at step 4.

## Questions about our process

### What happens after I request the audit?

We contact you to collect your brief first. We then audit your industry and your full funnel, pitch a strategy and action plan to fix the gaps, and share the prerequisites and the roadmap for onboarding and execution.

### Do I need to be running ads already?

No. The audit covers visibility, lead capture, nurturing, management and retention, and the form has an option for businesses that are not running ads yet.

### Where do the AI agents come in?

At step 3. The strategy names which manual steps our agents take over first, so automation starts where it saves the most effort.
