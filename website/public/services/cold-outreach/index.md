# Cold outreach that secures qualified leads and demos

Our cold outreach services cover data sourcing, email, WhatsApp and social DMs, and CRM automation, built to start real conversations with the right people.

t&f runs cold outreach from data sourcing to CRM automation across email, WhatsApp and social DMs, with the goal of qualified leads and booked demos.

## Outreach that starts conversations

Email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp outreach, followed up by agents and handed to your team when a person replies.

## What is included

### Data sourcing

Lists built around your ideal customer, with a record of where each contact came from.

### Email outreach

Sequences written for the segment, with a way to opt out.

### WhatsApp outreach

Messages that follow the platform's rules and read like a person wrote them.

### Social DMs

Personalised first messages on the channels your buyers use.

### CRM automation

Replies and follow-ups tracked in one place.

### Demo booking

The goal of every sequence: a conversation on the calendar.

Email, WhatsApp and social DMs, chosen by where your buyers respond.
