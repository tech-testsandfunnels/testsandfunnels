# Free Business Audit: find where your funnel loses leads and revenue

Tell us about your business. We collect your brief first, then audit your industry and your full funnel.

## What the audit covers

It starts with your industry, then follows your customer through five stages and marks which steps are still done by hand.

- **Visibility** Can the right people find you?
- **Lead capture** Do visits turn into enquiries?
- **Nurturing** Are leads followed up well and fast?
- **Management** Are leads and sales tracked and handled?
- **Retention** Do customers come back and recommend you?
