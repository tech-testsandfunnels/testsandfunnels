# Sales agents: every lead followed up, from first touch

Our AI sales agents bring leads in, follow them up on email and WhatsApp and run the workflows around tele-calls, so no enquiry waits for someone to remember it.

Our sales agents handle lead generation and management, follow-up by email and WhatsApp, and the workflows around tele-calls, so you can see which leads came from where and what happened to them.

## Three jobs, one lead list

### Lead generation and management

Brings leads in and keeps them organized.

- Capture from ads, forms and enquiries
- One list, not five

### Follow-up

Keeps every enquiry moving.

- Email and WhatsApp follow-up
- Follow-up that does not depend on memory

### Tele-calls

Tele-calling workflows around your leads.

- Who to call and when
- Notes and outcomes recorded against the lead

## Where leads get lost

- **Lead capture:** visits that never become enquiries.
- **Nurturing:** enquiries followed up late or not at all.
- **Management:** leads and sales not tracked in one place.
- **Retention:** customers who never hear from you again.

Our audit checks each stage and shows where you are losing leads and revenue. For human follow-up, see [sales services](https://testsandfunnels.com/services/sales-services/). The numbers from these agents feed the [operations agents](https://testsandfunnels.com/ai-automation/operations-agents/).

## Questions about sales agents

### Do the agents call my leads?

The tele-call agents run the workflows around calling your leads. People take the conversations that need a person.

### Can agents replace my sales team?

No. Agents handle the repetitive follow-up and the workflows around calls. People take the conversations that need a person, and our sales services cover hands-on lead conversion and training.
