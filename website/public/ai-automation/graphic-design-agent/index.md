# Graphic design agent: on-brand creatives, in every size you need

Our AI graphic design agent makes ad creatives, social posts, carousels, thumbnails and banners from your brand guidelines, in the variations your tests need and the sizes each placement needs, and a person reviews every design before it is used.

Our graphic design agent makes static ad creatives, social posts, carousels, thumbnails, banners and page graphics in your brand style, resized for each placement and varied for A/B tests. It works from your brand guidelines, and a person at t&f reviews every design before it is used.

## What the agent designs

### Ad creatives

Static ads for Meta, Google and LinkedIn.

- Variations of headline, image and offer for A/B tests
- Built from ads that already work
- Handed to the advertising agents to run

### Social posts and carousels

Posts planned in the content calendar.

- Single posts, carousels and story frames
- Templates that keep the feed consistent
- Scheduled by the social media agents

### Thumbnails and covers

The first frame people see.

- YouTube thumbnails and Reel covers
- Readable at small sizes
- Tested where the platform allows

### Website and landing page graphics

Visuals for the pages your ads land on.

- Banners, section graphics and icons
- Compressed for fast loading
- Matched to the campaign the page belongs to

## One design, every placement

- 1:1 feed
- 4:5 feed
- 9:16 stories and reels
- 16:9 video and web
- Banners

Each approved design is resized for every placement it will run in, so the text, logo and product stay inside the safe area on each one.

## How we keep it on brand and safe

- The agent works from your brand guidelines: logo, colours, fonts and templates.
- A person reviews every design before it goes into an ad or a post.
- Products are checked against the real product, so colours and details are accurate.
- AI-generated imagery is labelled where platform rules require it.
- For doctors, clinics and hospitals, we do not use AI imagery to show patients or treatment results.

Where the exact look of a product matters, we recommend a shoot: see [AI visuals and video](https://testsandfunnels.com/production-house/ai-visuals-and-video/) for when to use AI and when to shoot.

## Questions about the graphic design agent

### Does a graphic design agent replace a designer?

It handles first drafts, variations and resizing, which is most of the repeat work. A designer at t&f sets the direction and reviews every design before it is used.

### What if my brand has no guidelines?

We put them together first, as part of brand building: logo use, colours, fonts and a few templates. The agent then works from those.
