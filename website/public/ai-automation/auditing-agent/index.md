# Auditing agent: every stage of your funnel checked, manual steps marked

Our AI auditing agent runs the checks behind the Free Business Audit, from your visibility to your retention, and marks which steps are still done by hand, so the audit report reaches you faster and covers every stage.

Our auditing agent runs the checks behind the Free Business Audit: your industry, visibility, lead capture, nurturing, management and retention, and it marks which steps are still done by hand and could move to an agent. A person at t&f reviews the findings and presents the strategy.

## What the auditing agent checks

| Stage        | What the agent checks                                                                                                                                             |
|--------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Industry     | Market, competitors, customer segments and benchmark metrics, from the [market research agent](https://testsandfunnels.com/ai-automation/market-research-agent/). |
| Visibility   | Search results, AI answers, ad accounts, social profiles and local listings.                                                                                      |
| Lead capture | Landing pages, forms, offers, page speed, tracking and conversion events.                                                                                         |
| Nurturing    | Email and WhatsApp follow-up, automations and how fast enquiries get a reply.                                                                                     |
| Management   | How leads are handled and tracked, the CRM and the reporting.                                                                                                     |
| Retention    | Repeat purchase or re-engagement, reviews and loyalty.                                                                                                            |

## What you get

### An audit report

The gaps at each stage, with what was checked and what was found.

### The manual steps

Which steps are still done by hand and which could move to an agent first.

### A starting point

The strategy and action plan start from the report. See [how we work](https://testsandfunnels.com/how-we-work/).

## Agent checks, people decide

### What the agent does

It runs the same checks on every stage, every time, and drafts the findings. The same checks can be run again after changes, so you compare against the first audit.

### What stays with people

- Your brief, collected by a person before the audit starts.
- Reviewing every finding and adding what only a conversation can uncover.
- The strategy and action plan we pitch from the report.

## Questions about the auditing agent

### Is the Free Business Audit done by an AI agent?

The agent runs the checks and drafts the findings. A person at t&f collects your brief first, reviews the findings, adds what only a conversation can uncover and presents the strategy.

### What access does the auditing agent need?

Public checks, such as search, AI answers, social profiles and your website, need none. For ad accounts, analytics, Tag Manager or a CRM we ask for read access, and only where the audit needs it.
