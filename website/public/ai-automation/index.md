# AI agents that automate marketing, sales and operations

t&f is an AI automation agency in Delhi. Our agents handle the repetitive work across your marketing, your sales follow-up and your day-to-day reporting. You deal with one point of contact at t&f, and your budget goes into strategy and media, not coordination.

AI automation at t&f means agents that script, schedule, publish, set up and optimize ads, follow up leads, report, and keep dashboards current, working within limits you approve. People at t&f set the strategy and review what goes live.

## Agents do the repeat work

One person at t&f coordinates the agents and our team. Agents handle scripting, scheduling, follow-up and reporting inside limits you approve.

- Marketing, sales and operations agents
- Human review before anything goes live
- Reports that stay current

Our audit follows your customer from visibility to retention and marks which steps are still done by hand and could move to an agent. You get a plan that starts with the highest-effort steps, not a rebuild of everything at once.

## Questions about AI automation

### How is this different from a regular digital marketing agency?

A regular agency staffs each task with a person. We run the repetitive tasks with agents and keep people on strategy and review, so you manage one contact and less of your budget goes into execution.

### Will AI-written content sound generic?

Agents draft, and people review what goes live against your brand voice and the message templates you approve. Your point of contact owns the strategy, not the tool.

### Do I have to automate everything at once?

No. The audit marks which manual steps to move to agents first, and we begin there.

### Can AI agents automate sales and operations, not just marketing?

Yes. Sales agents handle lead capture, follow-up and the workflows around tele-calls. Operations agents keep MIS, lead dashboards, reports and plans current. Each starts from the steps the audit marks as manual.
