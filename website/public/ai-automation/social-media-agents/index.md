# Social media agents: from script to published post

Our eight AI social media agents run the whole workflow, so your channels stay active and your team is not chasing scripts, schedules and replies.

Our social media agents write scripts, plan the content calendar and production, edit video, publish on schedule, review performance, reach out to influencers and manage comments, within limits you approve.

## Eight agents, one workflow

### Scripting

Writes scripts for your videos, reels and posts in your brand voice, ready for review.

### Content calendar

Plans what is published and when, across your channels.

### Production plan

Lines up the shoots and assets the calendar needs, so production is planned before it is booked.

### Video editing

Edits your video content so it is ready to publish.

### Publisher

Publishes to your channels on schedule.

### Insights and data reviewer and optimizer

Reviews the performance data and tunes what goes out next.

### Influencer outreach

Reaches out to influencers on your behalf. See [influencer marketing](https://testsandfunnels.com/influencer-marketing/).

### ORM

Manages comments and responses on your channels. Conversations that need a person are passed to one.

## What you approve

- The content calendar and the topics it covers.
- The message templates the ORM agent replies from.
- Anything that makes a claim, especially in healthcare, which is checked by a person before it goes live.

Your point of contact at t&f reviews what goes live. The agents do the drafting and the repeating; they do not decide your strategy.

## Questions about social media agents

### Can the publisher post without my approval?

It publishes what has been approved. The calendar and the content are reviewed by your point of contact at t&f before they go out, and you set the limits the agents work within.

### Do agents reply to comments on their own?

The ORM agent handles comments and responses within templates you approve. Conversations that need a person are taken by a person.
