# Video editing agent: raw footage to reels, ads and cut-downs

Our AI video editing agent turns footage from shoots, UGC creators and screen recordings into reels, ad cuts and longer edits, with captions, music and your brand elements, in every format the channels need.

Our video editing agent cuts raw footage into reels, ads and longer edits: it trims, adds captions, music and brand elements, and makes a 16:9 master with 9:16 and other cut-downs, plus hook and length variations for testing. A person at t&f reviews every cut before it is published.

## What the agent edits

### Reels and shorts

Short-form video for Instagram, YouTube and LinkedIn.

- Tight cuts with a hook in the first seconds
- Captions burned in
- Covers from the graphic design agent

### Ad cuts

Versions of one ad for testing.

- Different hooks, lengths and captions
- Cut for each placement
- Results decide which version runs

### Longer edits

Brand films, explainers, podcasts and founder videos.

- A 16:9 master
- 9:16 and 1:1 cut-downs from the same footage
- Clips for the content calendar

## From footage to published

1.  ### Footage in

    From our [production house](https://testsandfunnels.com/production-house/), [UGC](https://testsandfunnels.com/production-house/ugc/) creators, your phone or screen recordings.

2.  ### First cut

    The agent selects takes, trims, and adds captions, music and brand elements.

3.  ### Review

    A person at t&f checks the cut against the brief, the brand and the platform's rules.

4.  ### Every format, then live

    The approved cut is exported for each placement and handed to the [social media agents](https://testsandfunnels.com/ai-automation/social-media-agents/) to publish, or the advertising agents to run.

## Rights and review

- Music and stock footage come from sources licensed for where the video will run.
- Creators and people on camera have agreed to how the footage is used.
- Paid collaborations are disclosed on screen and in the caption.
- Nothing is published until a person has reviewed the final cut.

## Questions about the video editing agent

### What footage can the video editing agent work with?

Footage from our own shoots, UGC creators, phone videos you record, screen recordings and existing brand videos. Longer footage can be cut into many short clips.

### Can it make several versions of one ad for testing?

Yes. It cuts the same footage into versions with different hooks, lengths and captions, and the test results decide which version keeps running.
