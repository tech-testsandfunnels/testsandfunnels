# Web development and SEO agent: pages built to rank, answer and convert

Our AI web development and SEO agent builds and maintains your pages and makes them easy to find in Google, in direct answers and in AI-generated answers (SEO, AEO, AIO and GEO), with tracking set up from day one.

Our web development and SEO agent builds websites and landing pages, keeps them fast and tracked, and does the technical SEO, answer engine optimization (AEO) and AI search optimization (AIO and GEO) work: titles, structured data, direct answers and files for AI crawlers. People review every page before it goes live.

## Two jobs, one agent

### Web development

Pages that are ready when the campaign is.

- Websites and landing pages on WordPress, Shopify or custom HTML
- Fast loading on mobile
- Tag Manager and analytics events set up before launch
- Landing page variations to test against each other

### SEO, AEO, AIO and GEO

Pages that search engines and AI answer engines can read, quote and attribute.

- Technical SEO: titles, descriptions, internal links, sitemaps
- Structured data (schema) for your business and pages
- A direct answer at the top of each page
- llms.txt, Markdown copies and AI crawler rules

- Rankings and clicks from Search Console.

No one can promise rankings or AI citations, and we do not. We show the work and the measurement.

## Questions about the web development and SEO agent

### What is the difference between AIO and GEO?

AIO is about appearing in Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode. GEO is about being understood and cited by generative AI engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Copilot. The same clear, structured, sourced pages help with both.

### Can an AI agent build my whole website?

Agents handle much of the build: pages, technical SEO, structured data and tracking. People set the structure and the design, and review every page before it goes live.
