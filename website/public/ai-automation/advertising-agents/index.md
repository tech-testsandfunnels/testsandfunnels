# Advertising agents for setup, optimization and reporting

Our AI advertising agents split the work: one sets up and keeps optimizing your campaigns, another reports on how your ads perform, stage by stage, from impressions to revenue.

Our advertising agents set up campaigns on Google, Meta and LinkedIn, keep optimizing them, and report on performance. You set the budget and audience limits; your point of contact at t&f reviews what goes live.

## Two agents, one job: ROI-positive ads

### Ads setup and optimizer

Sets up your campaigns and keeps optimizing them.

- Campaign and audience setup
- A/B and multivariate tests on ads, landing pages and messaging
- Ongoing optimization within your budget

### Ad reporting

Reports on how your ads are performing.

- Tracks the funnel: impressions, clicks, leads, sales, revenue
- Shows what each rupee of spend produced

## Where the agents run ads

- Google Ads
- Meta Ads
- LinkedIn Ads

The same agents work from the same funnel, so what you learn on one platform informs the next test. Strategy, budgets and creative direction stay with your point of contact and you. See [performance marketing](https://testsandfunnels.com/services/performance-marketing/) for how the campaigns are planned.

## Questions about advertising agents

### Who sets the ad budget?

You do. The agents work inside the budget and audience limits you approve, and your point of contact reviews what goes live.

### How do the agents test ads?

They run A/B and multivariate tests on ads, landing pages and messaging, and the results decide what runs next. This is the tests half of tests&funnels.
