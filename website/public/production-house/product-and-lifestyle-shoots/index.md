# Product and lifestyle shoots planned for your ad formats

Ad-ready e-commerce product photography and video for fashion, health and fitness and visual merchandising brands, shot to a plan that says where every asset will run.

We plan the shot list from the ad formats and funnel stages the assets will serve, produce in bulk, and edit into the cuts each channel needs.

## What the plan covers

- **Product stills and detail shots** for ads, your store and your landing pages.
- **Lifestyle and in-use shots** that show the product in the customer's world.
- **Short video** for feeds, stories and reels.
- **Variations** , so ads can be tested instead of guessed.

## From storyboard to cut-downs

1.  ### Plan

    The storyboard and shot list are tied to the funnel stage each asset serves: awareness, consideration or conversion.

2.  ### Shoot

    We produce in bulk against the list, so one session covers the whole plan.

3.  ### Edit and format

    Edits and cut-downs for each placement, delivered ready to upload.

## Questions about product and lifestyle shoots

### Can you shoot for the ads and the website in one session?

Yes. The plan lists where each asset will be used, including ads, landing pages and your store, so one session serves all of them.

### Do you shoot for fashion and lifestyle brands?

Yes. Fashion and lifestyle, e-commerce products, health and fitness and visual merchandising are all part of our production work.
