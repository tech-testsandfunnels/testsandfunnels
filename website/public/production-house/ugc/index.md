## How we run UGC

1.  ### Brief and script

    We start from the goal, the audience and the claim you want to make. Agents draft scripts and hooks, and a person reviews them.

2.  ### Creators

    We shortlist creators for fit with your audience. A person reviews the shortlist and briefs each creator, including disclosure and usage rights.

3.  ### Review

    Every video is checked against your brand and the advertising rules before it goes live.

4.  ### Cut and test

    We edit the content into the cut-downs your ads need and test hooks and angles against each other.

## Rights and disclosure

- Usage rights are agreed in the brief, including where and for how long the content can run as an ad.
- Paid collaborations are disclosed under ASCI's influencer advertising guidelines.
- For hospitals and clinics, no patient data and no treatment-outcome claims.

## Questions about UGC

### What is UGC?

UGC means user-generated content: content made by customers or creators in a natural, first-person style, rather than a studio ad. Brands use it in ads and on social because audiences tend to trust it more than polished brand creative.

### Can UGC run as a paid ad?

Yes, with the creator's permission, and usage rights are agreed in the brief. On Meta, partnership ads let a brand run an ad through a creator's handle.

### How many UGC videos do I need?

Enough to test several hooks and angles against each other. The plan sets the number from your goal and your budget, and the results decide which ones to make more of.
