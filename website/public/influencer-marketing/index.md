# Influencer marketing that is briefed, tracked and reported

t&f is an influencer marketing agency in Delhi. We find the right creators for your audience, reach out, brief them and track what each collaboration produces, so influencer spend is measured like any other channel.

t&f runs influencer marketing from shortlist to report: goal and audience, creator shortlisting and outreach, briefs and approvals, and tracking of clicks, leads and sales for each creator.

## From shortlist to report

1.  ### Define the goal

    What the campaign must produce, and who it must reach.

2.  ### Shortlist and reach out

    Our influencer outreach agent reaches out on your behalf. A person reviews the shortlist.

3.  ### Brief and approve

    Every brief covers disclosure under ASCI's influencer advertising guidelines, and content is approved before it goes live.

4.  ### Track and report

    Links and codes per creator show clicks, leads and sales, so you see what each collaboration produced.

## Formats we run

- Paid collaborations
- Product seeding
- Creator content reused in ads

Creator content can run as ads too. On Meta, partnership ads let a brand run ads through a creator's handle, with the creator's permission. See the [production house](https://testsandfunnels.com/production-house/) for how we plan content for reuse.

## Healthcare and regulated categories

Advertising rules limit what a creator can say about healthcare. For hospitals and clinics we keep creator content to education and awareness, with no patient data or treatment-outcome claims.

## Questions about influencer marketing

### How are creators chosen?

By fit with your audience and your goal, and by the quality of their content. A person reviews the shortlist before anyone is contacted.

### How do you measure influencer campaigns?

Each creator gets their own link or code, so clicks, leads and sales can be traced to the creator and the post.

### Can influencer content be used in my ads?

Yes, with the creator's permission, and we agree usage rights in the brief.
