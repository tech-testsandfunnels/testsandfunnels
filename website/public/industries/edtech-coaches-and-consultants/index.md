# Marketing for edtech, coaches and consultants: from first view to enrolled or signed

t&f is a marketing agency for edtech companies, coaches and consultants. We build the authority, the funnel and the follow-up that turn an audience into booked calls, enrolments and signed clients.

t&f runs founder and expert content, lead campaigns on Meta, Google and LinkedIn, webinar, video sales letter and landing page funnels, and follow-up on email and WhatsApp for edtech companies, coaches and consultants, measured by cost per lead, cost per booked call and how many leads enrol or sign.

## Three businesses, one kind of funnel

### Edtech and course creators

People compare options for weeks before they enrol, so the funnel has to teach and follow up.

- Free class, webinar or video sales letter
- Counselling call
- Enrolment, then referrals

### Coaches

People buy the coach before the programme, so trust is built in public first.

- Content that shows how you think
- A lead magnet or free session
- Discovery call, then the programme

### Consultants

Buyers are few and senior, so reach is targeted and every lead gets a person.

- Authority content on LinkedIn
- Targeted outreach to named accounts
- Discovery call, proposal, signed

## Questions from edtech companies, coaches and consultants

### Is a webinar or a video sales letter better for selling a course?

Both can work. We start with the one that fits your price and how much your buyers need to talk to someone before they pay, then test the other.

### Can you market a coach or consultant who has no audience yet?

Yes. Content builds the audience over time, so we run lead campaigns and, for consultants, targeted outreach alongside it, and the calendar does not wait for followers.

### Do you make the ads and the video sales letter?

Yes. Our production house shoots and edits the ads, the content and the video sales letter, and the same team runs the funnel they go into.
