## Performance marketing vs digital marketing

|             | Digital marketing                             | Performance marketing                |
|-------------|-----------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Focus       | Visibility, awareness and audience engagement | Measurable business outcomes and ROI |
| Measured by | Reach, engagement and followers               | Sales, revenue and ROAS              |

The program teaches both, and trains you to tie each to revenue.
